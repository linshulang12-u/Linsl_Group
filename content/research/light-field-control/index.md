Most of what the lab does starts here. A spatial light modulator lets us write
phase and angular dispersion onto a femtosecond pulse, turning a Gaussian focus
into a needle-like Bessel beam, an axially modulated beating pattern, or a
"light bullet" whose group velocity along the axis we choose.

In our Bessel light-bullet work, pump and Stokes bullets were given tunable,
opposite group velocities (as slow as about 0.1*c*), so that the position where
they overlap in the sample is set by their relative time of flight rather than
by moving the objective.
