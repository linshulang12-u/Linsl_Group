Our published work so far centres on **Bessel-beam optical sectioning** and
**stimulated Raman scattering (SRS) microscopy**: optical beating tomography,
ultraslow Bessel light bullets, and physics-paired self-supervised imaging.
We are now extending the same light-field control to **femtosecond laser
micro-fabrication** and optical monitoring of the machining process.
