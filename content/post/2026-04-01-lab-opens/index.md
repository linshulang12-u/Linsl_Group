In April 2026, Dr. Shulang Lin joined the College of Electronic Science and
Engineering at Jilin University as Professor and began setting up the lab.

<!--more-->

He comes from the National University of Singapore, where he completed his PhD
and postdoctoral research on Bessel-beam optical sectioning and stimulated
Raman scattering microscopy.
