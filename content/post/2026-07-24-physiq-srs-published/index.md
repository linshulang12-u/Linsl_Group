Our paper *Physics-paired stimulated Raman scattering microscopy enables
label-free phenotyping of lipid droplets 3D motility in live cells* is out in
*Light: Science & Applications*.

<!--more-->

PHYSIQ-SRS acquires co-registered in-phase and quadrature measurements that
carry matching Raman contrast and independent shot noise. This makes
self-supervised image restoration possible without clean training targets or
temporal averaging. The study reports approximately 12.5 dB SNR improvement and
label-free three-dimensional lipid-droplet tracking in live cells.
