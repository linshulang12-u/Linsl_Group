Shulang Lin is a Professor at the College of Electronic Science and Engineering,
Jilin University (since April 2026). Before that he was a postdoctoral researcher at the National
University of Singapore (2023–2026), where he also received his PhD in Optical
Engineering (2019–2024). He holds Bachelor's (2012–2016) and Master's
(2016–2019) degrees from Tsinghua University.

His first-author work includes Bessel light-bullet SRS microscopy, phase-shifted
optical-beating two-photon tomography, and physics-paired SRS imaging. Together,
these studies connect optical field design and signal acquisition with
depth-resolved imaging and quantitative measurements in living systems.
