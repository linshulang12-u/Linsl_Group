High-speed volumetric SRS imaging is limited by the trade-off between imaging speed and signal-to-noise ratio. PHYSIQ-SRS acquires co-registered in-phase and quadrature measurements carrying matching Raman contrast and independent shot noise. This enables self-supervised restoration without clean training targets or temporal averaging. The study reports approximately 12.5 dB SNR improvement and label-free three-dimensional lipid-droplet tracking in live cells.
